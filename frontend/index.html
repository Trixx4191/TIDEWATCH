⊕ SCIENTIFIC METHODOLOGY

Elevation Model: NASA SRTM v3.0 (SRTMGL1) — 1 arc-second (~30m) resolution, void-filled, reprojected to WGS84. Values converted from meters to feet.

Inundation Model: Coastal flood-fill BFS algorithm. Pixel inundated if: elevation_ft ≤ surge_height_ft + SLR_offset_ft AND hydrologically connected to ocean from coastline pixels. More accurate than pure threshold — disconnected inland depressions excluded.

Sea Surface Temperature: MODIS Level-3 4km daily composites via NASA OPeNDAP. Anomaly = deviation from 1985–2012 climatological baseline. SST >+0.5°C in Main Development Region correlates with intensification (Emanuel 1987, Webster et al. 1995).

Tidal Baseline: NOAA MHHW (Mean Higher High Water) — 19-year tidal epoch. All surge heights measured above MHHW.

SLR Scenarios: IPCC AR6 Table 9.9, Intermediate (SSP2-4.5): +0.30m by 2050, +0.56m by 2075, +1.01m by 2100.

Flood Equity Index: FEI = (% inundated land in low-income tracts) ÷ (% low-income tracts in region) × 100. Source: US Census ACS B19013.

Limitation: Bathtub model does not simulate wave dynamics or time-dependent surge. SLOSH or ADCIRC required for operational evacuation planning.
